## What is not measured yet

- Ingest throughput at fleet scale. The simulator drives one endpoint at
  a time; a multi-endpoint load generator is needed for a defensible
  events-per-second figure.
- Detection precision and recall. These require a labelled corpus of
  benign and malicious sessions, which does not exist yet. Quoting
  either number from the current scenario set would be meaningless.
